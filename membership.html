Questions before your first class.

Do I need to be in shape to start?

No. Every station has a screen showing the move, reps and timing, and the coach adjusts weights and options for you. People start here at every level.

How long is a class?

One hour, start to finish.

Which class should I try first?

Lift. It's the class Hardcore Fitness was built on. After one session your coach can point you to Fight Camp or Booty Camp.

What should I bring?

Water, a towel, and shoes you can lift and move in. Get here 10 minutes early for your first class.
