Your first hour,
minute by minute.

New to the gym? Here is exactly what happens, so nothing is a surprise.

  1. Get here early

    Sign in at the front desk and meet your coach.

  2. Tell the coach

    New, coming back from a break, or working around an injury? Say so. They'll set your weights.

  3. Follow the screen

    A screen at every station shows the move, your reps, sets and timing. No guessing.

  4. Leave with a plan

    Ask your coach which classes fit your week, and about meal plans in the HCF Diet App.
