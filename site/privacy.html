Who we are

This website belongs to Hardcore Fitness Winter Garden, 1401 Beulah Rd, Ste 100, Winter Garden, FL 34787. You can reach us at 407-993-7000 or gym email.

What we collect

When you sign up for a free class, we collect what you type into the form:

  • Your first and last name, email address and phone number
  • Your gender
  • Your ZIP code and a coupon code, if you add them
  • The class and time you pick, and any notes you add for the coach
  • Whether you agreed to receive text messages from us

We don't ask for payment details on this website.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your free class and confirm your spot
  • To answer your questions about classes and memberships
  • To send you texts about the gym and its offers, if you agreed to receive them

Text messages

If you agree to receive texts, we may text you about your class and about gym news and offers. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help, at any time.

Who we share it with

Sign-ups are stored in ClubReady, the system we use to manage members and visitors. ClubReady handles that information for us. Confirm whether Hardcore Fitness corporate can also see sign-ups. We don't sell your personal information.

Other services on this website

The map on this website comes from Google Maps, and our class schedule loads from Google. When your browser loads them, Google may receive basic information like your IP address, as described in Google's privacy policy.

Cookies and browser storage

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. It saves a copy of the class schedule in your browser so pages load faster on your next visit. The Google map may set its own cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep sign-up information as long as we need it to contact you and to keep our business records, unless you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you gave us. Call 407-993-7000 or email gym email, and we'll take care of it.

Children

This website isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has sent us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website or system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise complete security.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top.
